# Beauty & Cosmetics Store Inventory Management and Sales Reporting System

## 1. Business Problem

Beauty and cosmetics businesses manage multiple products, suppliers, sales channels, and stock movements. Without a structured reporting system, it can be difficult to monitor inventory levels, evaluate product profitability, identify replenishment needs, and make informed purchasing decisions.

This project was designed to transform business sales and inventory records into a practical management reporting system.

| Quantity Sold            |  1,366 |

*Values are displayed as rounded figures from the dashboard.*

## 3. Key Business Findings

### Sales and Profitability

The business recorded ₦11M in sales and ₦3M in profit, with a reported profit margin of 28%. This provides management with an overview of revenue generation and profitability.

### Product Performance

Sales varied across products, indicating the importance of reviewing individual product performance when planning purchases and evaluating stock movement.

### Category Profit Contribution

Skincare contributed the largest share of category profit at 26%, followed by Body Care (21%), Makeup (20%), Natural Care (19%), and Hair Care (14%).

### Sales Channels

Walk-in sales recorded the highest activity among the three channels displayed. This provides a starting point for reviewing channel performance and customer purchasing patterns.

### Payment Methods

Bank transfers recorded the highest activity among the payment methods displayed, highlighting the importance of accurate payment tracking and transaction reconciliation.

## 4. Business Recommendations

| Business Area           | Recommended Action                                                                                                |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Inventory replenishment | Use current stock, sales history, and supplier lead time to guide reorder decisions.                              |
| Product performance     | Review product-level sales and stock movement to identify replenishment priorities and slow-moving items.         |
| Profitability           | Monitor selling prices, purchase costs, and margins to support pricing and purchasing decisions.                  |
| Category performance    | Review category-level profit alongside product-level margins before adjusting purchasing or marketing priorities. |
| Expiry management       | Monitor expiry dates and prioritize timely action on products approaching expiry.                                 |
| Sales and payments      | Track channel performance and reconcile payment records to improve reporting accuracy.                            |

## 5. Reporting System Features

* Sales, orders, COGS, profit, and profit margin KPIs.
* Monthly revenue trend analysis.
* Sales channel and payment method reporting.
* Product-level sales performance.
* Category-level profit and cost analysis.
* Interactive filters for month, product, sales channel, category, brand, and expiry applicability.
* Inventory monitoring and management reporting.

## 6. Business Value

The system provides a structured view of sales performance and profitability while supporting inventory monitoring and purchasing decisions.

It demonstrates how business data can be transformed into actionable management information to help business owners monitor performance, identify areas requiring attention, and make more informed operational decisions.
